Privacy

Sadbot's Journey To Bliss can be played without an account. If you choose to sign in:

  • What we store: your email address (to send sign-in links) and your game progress: unlocked stages, best times and whether you finished the game.
  • What we don't store: passwords (there are none), payment details, location or tracking data. The game has no ads and no analytics.
  • Where it lives: with our backend provider, Supabase. Only you can read or change your progress.
  • Sign-in emails are sent only when you ask for one.
  • Delete your data: email the address on the game's GitHub page and we'll delete your account and progress.

Without signing in, progress is stored only in your own browser.
